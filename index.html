            Settings

            Online lookup

            Off by default. When on, for songs with missing info (no artist or album, a title taken from the file name, or no cover) the song's title, artist and album text and its length are sent to:

            • MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org), at most one request per second
            • Apple iTunes Search (itunes.apple.com), for cover art and genre

            Covers then load from Cover Art Archive (coverartarchive.org) or Apple. Your audio files never leave this device, and no account or key is used. Close matches only fill in info the file was missing; anything less certain waits for you to review. Results are saved in this browser so each song is looked up once.

            Downloads

            When a YouTube song starts playing, it and the next song are saved to the downloads folder as Artist - Title [id].m4a. Saved songs show up in your Library, start instantly and play offline. A song that's already saved isn't downloaded again.

            Add YouTube cookies

            Lets this app play YouTube videos as you, so YouTube stops asking if it's a bot.

            1. Install Cookie-Editor Open Web Store ↗ (opens in a new tab)
            2. In its extension settings, turn on Allow in Incognito
            3. Open a private (Incognito) window, go to youtube.com and sign in
            4. Click the cookie extension 🍪
            5. Click Export, then JSON (it's copied)
            6. Paste it here, or save it as a file and drop it on the box
            7. Close the private window without signing out, or YouTube signs these cookies out
            🍪 Drop the file here, or click and press Ctrl/⌘+V

            Tip: use a spare Google account. The cookies are kept as private cookies for this site and are only used to fetch videos.
